Is it for you

This is for you if

  • You're curious about kink or fetish and unsure where to begin
  • You want to express desires and boundaries more clearly
  • You'd like deeper connection with your partner(s) through practical strategies
  • You want to explore from an ethical, consensual, grounded place

This is not for you if

  • You're deciding whether your relationship is compatible long term, or trying to repair it. That is the work of a couples therapist.
  • You're in couples therapy working through trust issues
  • You're under 18

## What makes working with a behaviorist different

Most advice focuses on you as an individual. A behaviorist looks at the whole environment around you: your relationships, your routines and the situations you find yourself in. We identify the variables that make a behavior more or less likely to happen again under the same circumstances, then adjust them so the outcome you want becomes easier to reach.

That means we don't stop at insight. We build skills you can practice, and we set up your environment for success so change doesn't depend on willpower alone.
